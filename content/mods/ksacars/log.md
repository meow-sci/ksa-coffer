## 2026-10-10
* **Update**: Review pass against KSACars `1f0a84f`, StarMap `4dc60f8` and decompiled build 2026.10.10.5554. Lifecycle: [Coloured emissive lenses](features/coloured-emissive-lenses.md) and [Headlamps and tail lamps](features/headlights-as-spotlights.md) were labelled `frame:pre-render` (and `frame:lights`); their postfixes run inside `Program.Render` -> `UpdateRenderingResources`, so both are now `frame:render`. [Inject wheel forces from a Vehicle.PrepareWorker prefix](features/prepareworker-impulse-injection.md) now gives the exact `PrepareFrame` order by line (apply, input, nearest-orbit job, cloth dispatch and `PartTree` flushes, then `PrepareWorker` inside `ExecuteNextVehicleSolvers`, then `SyncGroundClutter` and the vehicle job), the per-vehicle window and the full `ClearHeldPlayerInput` conditions. The overview's `[StarMapUnload]` row and [one folder as both mods](features/mod-folder-content-and-code-mod.md) no longer say the unload hook removes patches (StarMap's id-less `UnpatchAll` has already removed them), the overview states that every StarMap frame hook runs after the next step is dispatched, and its patch table gives each patch's frame position. Accuracy: the terrain feature now settles `accurate` from `Celestial.GetTerrainHeightFromDirCcf` and shows the physics terrain (`TerrainPatch`, `BubbleTerrainGrid`) uses the default `true`; [mod log](features/mod-log-file.md) corrects the claim that KSA's logger is unreachable (`LogCategory.Make` is public); [engine sound](features/engine-sound-crossfade.md) notes that `StopAll` at unload is moot and when `PopulateSounds` runs; [scoops](features/scoop-colliders-and-loose-clutter.md) states the collider-write race precisely; [car panel](features/car-panel-and-modmenu.md) says which stage reads each control; the bridge notes that `spawn` stalls on the in-flight step. Three snippet headers and eight line anchors were corrected, `GlfwKeyEvent` is `RenderCore.Input`, and the bridge and MCP features use `file-ipc` instead of `inter-mod-api`. Added [Once-per-frame car step and craft registry](features/frame-step-latch-and-car-registry.md) and [Spawn a saved craft parked at a latitude and longitude](features/spawn-parked-craft.md). The overview features table was regenerated from frontmatter, and the catalog was synced.
