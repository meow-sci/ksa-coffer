## 2026-10-10
* **Update**: Independent review against OhMyStars `4bb602e`, StarMap `4dc60f8` and decompiled build 2026.10.10.5554. Added [Game-styled popups and toasts](features/game-styled-popups-and-toasts.md) (the `KSA.Popup` subclass and `TimedAlert` toasts, previously a paragraph of the binary switcher). [Attitude-mode hand-off patches](features/attitude-mode-handoff-patches.md): gauge buttons queue `InputEvents` and reach `SetEnum` at the next `frame:input` (not during `frame:draw-ui`); `SetNullRot` also runs on vehicle-solver workers through `FlightComputer.ComputeControl` on the worker's flight computer copy; added the `DisableOptimizations` note on inlining, the auto-burn bypass, uncontained patch-time failures and StarMap's id-less `UnpatchAll` at shutdown; enum rows link the enum files. [Orient a vessel to a star](features/orient-to-star.md): explains the `FlightComputer.CopyFrom` copy-back that forces the per-dispatch re-apply, and corrects the claim that a write after dispatch is racy (it is overwritten, not racy). [Star instance restyle patch](features/star-instance-restyle-patch.md) and [Star binary switcher](features/star-binary-switcher.md): the buffer is re-created whenever the requested capacity differs (always on the first apply), not only to grow; the old buffer could be retired through `RetiredResourceQueue`; `starsedit_settings.ini` is written only on preset save, use or delete; a saved binary selection is never re-applied at start-up (the old pitfall said it was applied when the window opens). [Save-scoped settings sidecar](features/save-scoped-settings-sidecar.md): overwrites are not missed, because `UncompressedSave.Overwrite` calls `Make`. [Star pointer](features/star-pointer.md): a target straight behind the camera goes to the top edge, not down. [Navball star marker](features/navball-star-marker.md): Sol uses its fixed catalog direction. [Occlusion by bodies](features/overlay-occlusion-by-bodies.md): description now says every body and body-orbiting vehicle of the system is tested. Smaller fixes in the sky overlay, settings, ModMenu and overview pages, five line-anchor ranges, the overview feature table and the catalog (list fields synced from frontmatter, summaries and how-to notes corrected, new topics).
