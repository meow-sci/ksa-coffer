## 2026-10-10
* **Update**: Game-loop and StarMap consistency review (StarMap 4dc60f8 `StarMap.Core/ModRepository/RuntimeMod.cs` L28, L108-L126; `StarMap.Core/Config/StarMapConfig.cs`). [purrTTY custom shell contract](features/purrtty-custom-shell-contract.md) said usable assemblies are "the intersection of the dependency's `ExportedAssemblies` and the importer's `ImportedAssemblies`". At the pinned StarMap `RuntimeMod.ExportedAssemblies` is never populated from `mod.toml`, so the importer's `ImportedAssemblies` alone decides, which is why the contract is shared (gatOS lists both assemblies) and why purrTTY's `ExportedAssemblies` line has no effect; the bullet now says so, matching the StarMap and purrtty bundles.
* **Update**: Review pass against gatOS b88c285, StarMap 4dc60f8 and build 2026.10.10.5554. Lifecycle fixes: [ModMenu entry with a fallback menu](features/modmenu-entry-with-fallback-menu.md) now names `Program.OnDrawUiThreadSafe` (not `OnDrawUiViewports`) as the caller of `DrawMenuBar` and describes the separate "Menu Bar" window; the `DrivePerFrame` ticks (audio, paint, clutter textures, stickers, thug-life) are placed in `StarMapBeforeGui` or the hidden-HUD `StarMapAfterOnFrame` fallback, never the main-viewport prefix, in [FMOD audio playback](features/fmod-audio-playback.md), [Vehicle and EVA paint](features/vehicle-and-eva-paint.md), [Ground-clutter texture override](features/clutter-texture-override.md), [Projected sticker decals](features/sticker-projected-decals.md) and [World-space textured quad (thug_life)](features/thug-life-world-space-quad.md), with the one-frame consequence on hidden-HUD frames; render-data postfixes and prefixes are `frame:render`, not `frame:pre-render`, in [Vehicle and EVA paint](features/vehicle-and-eva-paint.md), [Per-vessel scale and force-render cheats](features/scale-and-force-render-cheats.md) and [Part light cone control](features/light-cone-control.md); the solver prefix is `frame:dispatch-solvers` in [Solver-phase drain for flight computer setpoints](features/solver-phase-command-drain.md), [Debug cheats for teleport, refuel, warp and impulse](features/debug-cheats-teleport-refuel-warp.md) and [Game-thread command queue](features/game-thread-command-queue.md). [Welds that pin one vessel to another](features/welds-vessel-anchoring.md), [IVA cabin physics in an own Bepu world](features/iva-cabin-physics-bepu.md) and [Frame hooks with an F2-proof fallback](features/frame-hooks-and-f2-fallback.md) now state that the solver step is still running at `StarMapAfterGui` (gatOS's own comment is wrong), that `VehicleSolver.Wait()` stalls the main thread, that it does not make `Vehicle` reads fresher, and that welds survive the next apply because `Vehicle.Teleport` removes the source from its bubble. Unload notes now say `StarMapCore.DeInit` has already run `Harmony.UnpatchAll()` before `StarMapUnload`, and the [gatOS](overview.md) hook table says the zero-argument `[StarMapImmediateLoad]` method is never invoked. Engine fixes: `Universe.SetSimulationSpeed` has no auto-warp refusal (only the console command does); `KSA.Atmosphere.Rendering.CloudRenderer` and `RenderCore.RenderTechnique` namespaces; the camera director follows its own `IFollowable`, not the target; new pitfalls for the `NearestOrbitPointJob` race on teleports. Added [Synthetic camera follow anchor](features/synthetic-camera-follow-anchor.md) and its catalog entry; corrected line anchors in several features.
