# KSAStructures bundle history

## 2026-10-11
* **Update**: Cross-link pass added See-also links from feature pages to the how-to articles that cite them in `demonstrated_by` (`scripts/content_crosslink.ts`).
* **Update**: [Generated deep ramp](features/generated-deep-ramp.md) no longer claims static-object colliders can only be boxes; the engine accepts capsule, cylinder, sphere, convex-hull and mesh collider templates, box is this mod's choice.

## 2026-10-10

* **Update**: Independent review against KSAStructures c2475935, StarMap 4dc60f8 and the decompiled KSA build 2026.10.10.5554. Lifecycle: the `LocationReference.UpdateStaticObjectRenderData` postfix runs inside `Program.Render` -> `UpdateRenderingResources`, so [launch-pad landmarks](features/office-landmarks-static-objects.md), [the lift](features/lift-moving-landmark.md), [garage spot lights](features/garage-spot-lights.md), [invisible shadow casters](features/invisible-shadow-casters.md) and the [overview](overview.md) now say `frame:render`, not `frame:pre-render`; [terrain decal pits](features/terrain-decal-pits.md) lost a spurious `frame:dispatch-solvers`; [frame step plumbing](features/frame-step-plumbing.md) now states that every step hook runs after solver dispatch and that `StarMapUnload` runs after StarMap's id-less `UnpatchAll`. Settled from code: terrain-modifier render data and the `PlanetRenderer` modifier buffer are sized once in the `Program` constructor and never resized; `AltitudeOffset`'s `Km` attribute is read raw and used as metres on CPU and GPU; Earth's stock modifiers use nine of the sixteen texture slots; clutter exclusion zones are baked into the ecotype buffers once at load against the load-time camera; KSA's menu bar is a `"Menu Bar"` window with `BeginMenuBar` in every build in the history including 2026.10.7.5541, and `Program.DrawProgramMenusHook` is the way into it ([settings menu](features/settings-menu.md)); the shader-reload "destroy and keep nothing" path is unreachable ([developer bridge](features/dev-file-bridge-and-mcp.md)); secondary viewport surfaces rebuild in `OnFrameViewports`, before the step ([monitor image](features/viewport-image-on-monitor.md)). Fixed three wrong line anchors and a wrong "worker-thread" description of the terrain height patch in [dry volume](features/dry-volume-below-sea-level.md). Added [ground clutter exclusion zones](features/ground-clutter-exclusion-zones.md), [noticing a save load](features/save-load-detection.md) and [live terrain material override](features/live-terrain-material-override.md), split out of the landmark, frame-plumbing and decal concepts. The lifecycle reference's `OnPreRender` and `Render` rows were corrected in the same pass.
